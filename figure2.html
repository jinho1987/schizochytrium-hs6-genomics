Figure 2. Trans-regulatory and metabolic architecture of the HS6 phenotype relative to parent strain #4 (WT). Panel 1: nine MYB/MYB3R-family transcription factors and one CDK-like kinase, coordinately dysregulated in HS6 versus WT, share a common cis-regulatory motif (STREME de novo discovery, P = 3.2×10⁻⁷; independently reconfirmed using StringTie-derived promoter boundaries). The same motif occurs in the promoters of three pathway genes in panels 2–3 (dashed arrows), consistent with — but not proof of — direct transcriptional regulation. Panels 2–4 show the fatty-acid/PKS, triacylglycerol, and MVA-pathway backbone with each gene colored by its own log2 fold change (HS6 vs. WT #4, DESeq2, n = 1 per genotype × timepoint; dashed node borders indicate padj ≥ 0.05). No coding mutation was identified in any regulator or pathway gene shown — every gene in this figure was screened against high-confidence WT #4 resequencing variants and found unmutated. Mevalonate kinase (panel 4, g10519) additionally carries a 2-fold copy-number duplication (Scaffolds_28) that is present in both WT #4 and HS6 (confirmed from WT #4's own short-read depth at this locus), indicating the duplication predates HS6's derivation and is not itself a driver of the WT-to-HS6 phenotypic change.
Differential expression: DESeq2, design ~genotype + time, n = 1 per genotype × timepoint (statistics reflect pooled-dispersion estimation, not biological replication). Motif discovery: STREME (MEME Suite) on StringTie-derived promoter boundaries, up- and down-regulated gene sets (padj < 0.05, |log2FC| > 0.5) vs. genome-wide background. Variant screening: BWA-MEM + bcftools (haploid model), WT #4 resequencing reads vs. HS6 reference, high-confidence filtered call set. † Myb-related A (g10428) resides on Scaffolds_28, the same locus as MVK. Full analysis: HS6 vs. WT #4 RNA-seq/variant report and HS6 vs. Schizochytrium sp. #7 comparative genomics report.
