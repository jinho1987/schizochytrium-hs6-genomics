Figure 1. Comparative metabolic map of lipid, isoprenoid, and respiratory-chain biosynthesis in Schizochytrium sp. HS6 and strain #7. (A) Fatty acid synthesis via the polyketide synthase route to PUFA/DHA. (B) Triacylglycerol assembly and turnover (Kennedy pathway), including the β-oxidation branch. (C) The mevalonate (MVA) pathway supplying isoprenoid units to sterol and carotenoid biosynthesis. Gene identifiers and pairwise amino-acid identities (italic enzyme name, locus ID, percent identity) are shown above each arrow, derived from reciprocal-best-hit orthology between the two independently PacBio HiFi–assembled genomes (98.9% genome-wide nucleotide identity; see Methods). Of the 15 enzymatic steps directly evidenced by the gene panel, 14 are single-copy orthologs at 96.5–100% identity in both strains. The sole exception is mevalonate kinase (MVK, g10519, red), which resides on Scaffolds_28 — the one locus in this comparison carrying an independently confirmed 2-fold copy-number duplication specific to HS6 (79.5× vs. 42.9× long-read depth, HS6 vs. #7 — each strain's own HiFi reads independently self-mapped to its own assembly at the orthologous locus; genome-wide baseline ≈ 41× HS6 / 43× #7, inset). Dashed grey arrows in (C) denote canonical MVA-pathway and downstream isoprenoid steps (HMG-CoA reductase, phosphomevalonate kinase, and the sterol/carotenoid branch point) that are biochemically expected but not directly represented among the DEG/orthology panel analyzed in this study. (D) A respiratory-chain/ubiquinone module — Complex I (NDUFS4, NDUFS5, NDUFA8, NDUFB7, assembly factors NDUFAF1/NDUFAF3), Complex II (SDHC, SDHAF4), ubiquinone biosynthesis (COQ9), and Complex III assembly (UQCC5) — was initially flagged as HS6-specific by the independent, genome-wide KEGG-ortholog comparison. Targeted verification (DIAMOND blastx of all ten HS6 proteins against the #7 genome, unrestricted target reporting) recovered a match for every gene, six of them full-length at 90–100% amino-acid identity; the #7 genome's own MAKER annotation independently calls and names the same loci (e.g. Ndufaf3, Coq9) at the matching coordinates. The apparent absence therefore traces to the KEGG-ortholog (KO) assignment step of the comparison pipeline, not to a gene-content or metabolic difference — the same class of false negative previously identified and corrected for dgat2_1 in the main comparative genomics report. This module is retained here, marked as tested and excluded, rather than silently removed, so the negative result is on record. Complex IV/ATP synthase (grey, dashed) shows a mixed, non-directional signal (COX5B/COA7 on the HS6 side vs. COX17 on the #7 side) and was never scored as strain-specific. Of the growth-associated candidates considered for the higher biomass accumulation of HS6 relative to #7 under glucose and glycerol provision, only the MVK/mevalonate-pathway copy-number difference in (C) — confirmed independently by long-read depth realignment — currently stands as genome-verified evidence.
Orthology: bidirectional DIAMOND blastp, reciprocal best hit. Genome alignment: minimap2 (asm10). Depth: each strain's own raw HiFi reads independently realigned to its own assembly (minimap2, map-hifi) and depth measured at the orthologous MVK locus (HS6 Scaffolds_28; #7 contig26, LOCUS_013099) versus that strain's own genome-wide baseline — a symmetric check on both sides, not a comparison against a reported/assumed figure. Panel D: independent KEGG orthology (KO) assignment per strain via the KEGG REST API (UniProt → KEGG GENES → KO), compared as presence/absence across the full 3-way KO matrix (3,938 KOs); the ten flagged genes were then verified by DIAMOND blastx of the HS6 proteins against the #7 genome (--max-target-seqs 4100) and cross-checked against the #7 MAKER gene models directly. Pathway topology follows KEGG reference maps 00061, 00071, 00561, 00900, 00190. Full analysis: HS6 vs. Schizochytrium sp. #7 comparative genomics report.
